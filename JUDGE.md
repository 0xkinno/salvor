# JUDGE.md

Three paths on the live site. No login. The same text is on `/judge`.

## 60 seconds

1. `/` Read the hero: a real item, what was paid, what it costs today, what is owed, labelled with the run it came from.
2. `/app` Open Teresa's receipts. Each card names its own Sprite, days left, last wake and next wake.
3. `/try` Paste your own Target or Best Buy link, the price you paid and the date. A pooled Sprite reads the page live. Caps: 3 an hour in total, 1 per visitor.

## 3 minutes

1. Open the receipt with money owed. Read the then and now ladder, the match scores and the store's own clause with its source and checked date.
2. Press **Verify this claim**. Salvor reads Fly's own checkpoint copy of the evidence and shows both hashes.
3. `/fleet` Every Sprite with its label, one-name network rule, checkpoints and CPU time, read from Fly now.
4. `/proof` What is measured and what is not, break tests with failures kept.

## 10 minutes

1. `/how` The diagram, where intelligence is and is not, the delete test for each Sprite feature.
2. `/proof` Curated and wild rates apart, Pinecone against plain word overlap on the same lines, cost per receipt from Fly's meter.
3. `/api/status` Raw measured status with timestamps. `/api/stats` recomputes the headline from the ledger.
4. Trigger a failure: on a receipt with a question, answer "a different one", or give `/try` a link to a different variant. Salvor drops the claim and says why.
5. Offline proof: `node scripts/verify-claim.mjs <url> <claim id>` re-verifies a claim from a terminal.

## What is deliberately not here

No login, no wallet, no card. A claim email goes only to the owner's test inbox during the hackathon.
